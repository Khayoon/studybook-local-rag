# A small, repeatable language study plan

This is an original demonstration document for Studybook. It describes a practice routine, not a professional examination standard.

## The weekly loop

Set aside three short practice sessions each week. In the first session, read a short passage and collect unfamiliar phrases. In the second, explain the passage aloud in another language. In the third, review difficult phrases and record one new attempt.

Use a learning journal with four columns: date, source passage, difficulty, and next action. The next action should be small enough to complete in one session, such as practicing two number sequences or paraphrasing a single paragraph.

## Choose an achievable target

A useful target describes an observable action: "I can explain the main point and two supporting details of this passage." A target such as "become fluent" is too broad to check after a short session.

For this demonstration, the suggested session length is 20 minutes. Spend five minutes preparing, ten minutes practicing, and five minutes reviewing. Change the timing to suit your own energy and schedule.

## Review together

Compare your first and most recent attempts. Identify one improvement and one issue to revisit. A study partner can help by describing what they understood, rather than assigning a grade.

## Connection to the other demonstration sources

The glossary notebook supplies phrases for the preparation stage. The interpreting practice guide provides activities for the ten-minute practice stage. Use the learning journal to connect both.
